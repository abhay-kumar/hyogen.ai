# FFmpeg Final Assembly with Remotion Graphic Shots

The final video will be assembled with FFmpeg, while Remotion is used for generated graphic, chart, diagram, and data-card shots rather than as the whole-video renderer. This favors deterministic media handling, robust audio/caption composition, and F1.ai-derived rendering lessons over a more general web-animation pipeline; the trade-off is less whole-video animation flexibility in the MVP.
